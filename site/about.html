About Me

Meet the personbehind the design.

A little about who I am and how I like to work with the people I design for.

My Story

I believe a space should feel like it was made for the person who lives in it.

I start by listening. Before I draw anything, I want to understand how you move through your day, what you enjoy about your space and what gets in the way. Those small details shape every decision that follows.

I plan clearly and explain my thinking. You will always know why a layout, a material or a colour has been chosen, and you are welcome to question it. Good design should make sense to the person who lives with it.

I like to show the result before it is built. Realistic 3D previews let us test ideas, adjust them and agree on a direction together, so there are fewer surprises later on.

Whether it is a single room or a whole home, I aim for the same thing: spaces that feel calm, work well and stay true to you rather than to a passing trend.
